Total, free and bioavailable testosterone

  • Total testosterone measures all the testosterone in the sample: the part bound to proteins in the blood and the small part that isn't.
  • Free testosterone measures only the part that isn't bound to proteins.
  • Bioavailable (free and weakly bound) testosterone measures the free part plus the part loosely bound to a protein called albumin.

These are definitions only. Which test to order and what a result means are questions for a licensed clinician.

How ordering works

  1. Choose the test on the seller's website and pay online. Some sellers add an order or physician fee; the totals above include it where the seller lists it.
  2. The seller gives you a lab order, usually by email or in your account.
  3. Go to the Quest or Labcorp patient service center the seller names. Some take walk-ins; others ask you to book a time.
  4. The seller posts your results to your account.

Not every seller can sell tests in every state, and each has its own rules. Check the seller's site before you pay. TRT Ledger doesn't sell tests, and no seller pays to be listed here.
