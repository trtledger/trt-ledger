Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Augusta?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Georgia start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Augusta, GA?

19 clinic listings in Augusta: 10 urology practices and 9 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in Georgia?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 5 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Georgia.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
