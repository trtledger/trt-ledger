Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Pasadena?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Texas start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. In person, Low T Center lists $25 first test · $135 confirmation + first shot · ~$105.50/mo injections · $135 follow-ups. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Pasadena, TX?

Can I get TRT online in Texas?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 4 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Texas.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
