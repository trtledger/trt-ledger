Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Greenville?

Online clinics that confirm they serve South Carolina start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Greenville, SC?

15 clinic listings in Greenville: 2 TRT or men's health clinics, 8 urology practices and 5 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in South Carolina?

Yes. PeterMD and 2 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in South Carolina.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
