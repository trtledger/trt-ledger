Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Columbus?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Ohio start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. In person, Low T Center lists $25 first test · $135 confirmation + first shot · ~$105.50/mo injections · $135 follow-ups. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Columbus, OH?

37 clinic listings in Columbus: 4 TRT or men's health clinics, 22 urology practices and 11 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in Ohio?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 5 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Ohio.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
