Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Pittsburgh?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Pennsylvania start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Pittsburgh, PA?

48 clinic listings in Pittsburgh: 27 urology practices and 21 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in Pennsylvania?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 4 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Pennsylvania.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
