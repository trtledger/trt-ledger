Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Colorado Springs?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Colorado start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. In person, Low T Center lists $25 first test · $135 confirmation + first shot · ~$105.50/mo injections · $135 follow-ups. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Colorado Springs, CO?

19 clinic listings in Colorado Springs: 3 TRT or men's health clinics, 1 hormone clinic, 11 urology practices and 4 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in Colorado?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 6 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Colorado.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
