Common questions

How much does TRT cost in North Dakota?

Online clinics that confirm they serve North Dakota start at about $708 for the first year (Talon Wellness), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in North Dakota?

Yes. Talon Wellness and 3 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in North Dakota.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
