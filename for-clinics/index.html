For clinics

TRT Ledger compares what patients really pay in year one. Clinics can work with us in the ways below, and none of them changes where a clinic ranks.

Free, always

  • Fix or add your listing

    If a price is wrong, missing or out of date, send us the page where it's published. We check it and update the site, usually within a week.

    Email us

Paid options

  • “Claimed by clinic” badge

    Your clinic confirms its own pricing with us directly, and we show a badge next to your name. Rankings still sort by true first-year cost only.

    Email us

  • Market pricing report

    A pricing report is in preparation using the published prices tracked by this site. Email us to ask about availability and scope.

    Email us

What money never buys

  • A higher ranking or a “best” label.
  • Hiding a fee, or removing or burying a competitor.
  • Changing how we calculate first-year cost.

Paid relationships are disclosed next to the clinic they apply to.
