For clinics

TRT Ledger compares what patients really pay in year one. This page explains how your listing works, how to fix it for free, and what the paid options do and don't include. Nothing on this page changes where a clinic ranks.

Your listing

Every online clinic with published prices has its own page with the price we show, the first-year math line by line, the contract terms, and a link to the page on your site where we read each price. Find your clinic's page.

  • Where prices come from. We read them from your own website, every day for most clinics and at least once a week for the rest, and show the date of the last check.
  • How the first-year total is built. Twelve months of your listed price, plus any intake or startup fee, plus labs and medication if they are billed separately. If you don't publish one of those costs we use the same stated assumption for every clinic ($40/month for medication, $150 lab panels 4 times a year) and mark the total as an estimate.
  • Plans with several terms. When the price depends on the term (for example monthly, 6-month and 12-month prepaid), the listing uses the lowest published price, shows what must be paid up front to get it, and lists the other terms.
  • Promotions. We list standing published prices. Short-term promotions and coupon codes aren't used in the math.

Free, always: fix or add your listing

You never need to buy anything to correct your listing or keep it accurate. That applies to every clinic, whether or not it pays for anything.

  1. Email us the address of the page on your website where the correct price or term is published.
  2. We check the page and update the listing, usually within a week.
  3. If the price isn't on your website yet, you can send it in writing. We can show it as a labelled “clinic-provided estimate”, listed after clinics with published prices, until it is on your site.

We change facts: prices, fees, terms, what's included, states served. We write the listing ourselves, so clinics can't add marketing copy or remove a fee or a caution.
