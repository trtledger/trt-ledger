      Frequently asked questions

      How to read the prices, check the details, and use the guide.

      How do you calculate the first-year cost?

      We add 12 months of the listed plan price, published startup fees, medication, and labs. Missing medication and lab costs use the assumptions shown above the results. Unpublished intake fees are excluded and flagged, so the actual total may be higher. Open a clinic’s cost breakdown to see the math and check any prepayment terms.

      Are these prices a quote from the clinic?

      No. These are comparisons based on published prices, with estimates where details are missing. Your prescription, tests, shipping, and plan terms may change the amount you pay. Confirm the full price directly before signing up.

      What does “Check your state” mean?

      We haven’t confirmed that the clinic serves your state. Those clinics are shown separately from confirmed options. Ask the clinic before paying; appearing in the guide does not confirm eligibility.

      Why isn’t every clinic ranked?

      Some clinics do not publish enough pricing information to calculate a comparable total. We keep those in a separate list instead of inventing a price. In-person directory entries are locations, not endorsements or confirmation that every provider offers TRT.

      Can a clinic pay for a higher ranking?

      No. Payment does not change the comparison order, cost calculations, or lowest-cost labels. There are currently no affiliate links on TRT Ledger.

      Do you store my ZIP code or calculator answers?

      Your calculator answers are saved in your browser so you can return to them. ZIP searches and comparisons run in your browser. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and GoatCounter to count visits, and GoatCounter also counts clicks on links to clinic websites. Neither uses cookies or builds a profile of you.; signing up for price alerts sends your email to our email service.

      How can I suggest a clinic or correct a price?

      Email teamtrtledger@gmail.com with the clinic name and a link to its published pricing. Please don’t send medical records, lab results, or other private health information.

      About TRT Ledger

      TRT Ledger is an independent price guide built by a TRT patient who wanted a clearer way to compare costs. A monthly headline rarely tells the whole story, so this guide brings startup fees, labs, medication, and plan terms together.

      We link to sources and label missing information. We compare costs, not the quality of care, and we are not a clinic or a substitute for a licensed clinician.

      How we get our data

      • Most online clinic prices are re-read from the clinic's own website every day; the rest are checked by hand at least once a week. Every listing shows its last check date.
      • In-person clinics come from the federal NPI registry and clinic chains' own location lists, refreshed monthly.
      • ZIP code locations come from the U.S. Census Bureau.

      Important

      TRT Ledger is not medical advice and is not a pharmacy or clinic. Prices change; confirm with the provider before you pay. Testosterone is a prescription medication — talk to a licensed clinician.
