Clinics that do not publish full prices

  • Evolve Telemed — Free consult; pricing only after you talk to an intake coordinator
  • Ways2Well — Care-plan tiers include labs; no prices on site. Not in MO, NJ, NY, RI
  • Keeps TRT (formerly Titan) — $49 to start (labs + consult); monthly price only shown inside its sign-up quiz
  • Ehormones MD — Prices not published on its site
  • Huddle Men's Health — Prices not published; telehealth in CA and WA
  • Cuda Health — Pay per vial, no membership (labs, then a consult); testosterone prices not published on its site
  • Hims — Injectable TRT medication starts at $79/month; oral TRT at $179/month. A separate membership is required, but its price is not shown on the public page. A complete first-year cost cannot be verified.
  • Joi + Blokes — The current TRT page describes clinician visits, medication delivery and quarterly labs, but does not display a price. The previous $99/month figure could not be reconfirmed.
  • Maximus — Offers testosterone cream and other treatment options. The current official cream page does not publish plan prices; previous third-party pricing is not used for ranking.
  • H4M Wellness (HormonesForMe) — Custom wellness plans start at $235 per 3 months, with free consult and onboarding; labs are separate. The page does not establish a complete testosterone-specific cost, so this is not ranked.
  • Ryze HRT — Online, all 50 states. Men's HRT "starting as low as $85/month"; what that includes and lab costs are not published, so no first-year total yet
  • Gatlan Health — Testosterone "as low as $125/mo", but what that includes, lab costs and states aren't published
  • Thrivelab — Offers compounded TRT and takes some insurance; prices not published on its site
  • Taurus Medical — Free testosterone test to start; TRT prices not published on its site
